About

I build things with AI — and I run every line of it through a human first: me.

I’m a builder, creator, streamer, programmer, and IT generalist in Perry County, PA. I founded MYTHOS Corporation as the umbrella for everything I make: Troll Squad Productions (media & production), Mythos Interactive (games), Mythos Manufacturing (physical products), and Mythos Systems (client builds).

It started with a team. I founded Troll Squad as a gaming team and built it into Troll Squad Productions, a production company — the team still plays, and the company produces: streaming, broadcast production, merch, and a six-page content network run on a planned weekly system.

My flagship client build: a complete digital stack for Zeiderelli’s Pizza & Subs in Duncannon, PA — a live business website, a custom point-of-sale system with zero middleman fees, an owner control panel, and a kiosk terminal converted from a 2015 all-in-one PC. The system ships with 496 automated checks, all passing.

My flagship creation: MYTHOS: Hostile Takeover, a corporate-horror trading card game — a 60-card set designed end to end, a live playable browser game, and a physical edition where every card links back to the digital game by QR code and NFC.

I also run a six-page Facebook network on a planned weekly content system with per-page monetization strategies, and I’m producing The Credential Gauntlet, a 12-episode series climbing the Microsoft and Google certification ladder on camera — free badges first, every dollar of paid exams disclosed.

My angle is simple: everyone’s shipping AI output raw. I break it, fix it, and ship what survives.

Open to remote tech/IT work and client builds.
